About

Product
Designer.

I start with the problem, not the pixels.

I'm Omar, a product designer from Cairo. I came to design the long way: I studied accounting at Helwan University, then moved into UX and sharpened it through ITI's intensive UI/UX program.

Five-plus years on, I've designed B2B and B2C products for teams like STC, Momentasoft, Adree and Seam Labs: SaaS platforms, CRMs, fintech apps and logistics tools, across web and mobile.

I own the full UX lifecycle, from discovery and user flows to high-fidelity UI, and I build design systems that keep products consistent and ship faster. Much of my work is bilingual, so Arabic and RTL are first-class, not an afterthought.

How I work

  1. 01

    Understand

    Talk to users and stakeholders, read the data, and agree on the problem worth solving before any screens.

  2. 02

    Map

    Turn it into user flows, information architecture and edge cases, so the team sees the whole product, not just the happy path.

  3. 03

    Design

    Prototype, test and refine high-fidelity UI built on a shared design system, in English and Arabic.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Hand off clean specs, stay close to engineering through build, and measure what changed after launch.
